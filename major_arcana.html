Tarot Magic - Major Arcana

varpeti

0 The Fool

Teleport the caster to the last room they left. If the room is more than 100m away, just teleport 100m to that direction in the nearest safe spot.

I The Magician

Draw 5 cards, keep the lowest 2, burn the rest.

II The High Priestess

Summon an invulnerable white-gold Pegasuscat. Each creature next to the Pegasuscat (1 Space) has +1 for Attribute and Damage rolls. The Pegasuscat has 3 Speed, and can be commanded to move as a Small Action.

III The Empress

Roll 1d4 suffer this as true damage, the closes ally gain this many HP (up to their maximum HP)

IV The Emperor

Roll 1d10. Gain a Golden Apple: When eaten removes 1 Wound and place it to the rolled slot in your Backpack. If there is an item on that slot drop it to the floor. If there is a condition on that slot remove it.

V The Hierophant

The caster freezes in loud prayer to their god for at least 5 minutes. They are invulnerable until they finish the prayer, but needs air, and not unmovable. If the prayer interrupted take 1 wound.

VI The lovers

Chose 2 creature, they gain the Lovers Condition Clear: 10 minutes. They cannot harm each other in any way. If they accidentally do harm, the harm automatically applies to the attacker instead of the victim.

VII The Chariot

When the caster move, move everyone around (1 Space) as well, if they are not blocked by walls or other living beings. Size does not matter.

VIII Justice

In 8 Space around the caster, if anyone harms another creature they harm themself the same way for 10 minutes.

IX The Hermit

Everyone becomes invisible around the caster (3 space) until the caster moves or does any action.

X Wheel of Fortune

Roll on the d666 table. Immediately cast that spell. This does not cost any extra AP or card and automatically succeed.

XI Strength

Everyone in 8 Space around the caster gains +1 Strength for 10 minutes Condition drop at will.

XII The Hanged Man

The caster’s body disappears and becomes a floating head, it cannot fly higher or gain height in any way, but can keep the height. Can float at normal walking speed. Can turn back to normal at will.

XIII Death

Choose a number between 3 and 10, everyone around the caster in 8 Space suffers this many true damage, including allies and the caster.

XIV The Tower

Everyone including the caster and it’s allies in 8 Space roll 1d20 and teleports directly up, but maximum at the ceiling.

XV The Devil

Make a deal with the Devil:

  • Permanently lose 1 slot from the Backpack and gain +1 Attribute
  • Permanently lose 1 Attribute and gain +1 slot in the Backpack

XVI Temperance

Gain the Hungry Condition, cannot eat for a day (24 hour). But receives +2 STR, +1 WIL and +1 DEX. If you eat you die.

XVII The Stars

Burn all your cards and draw as many +1.

XVII The Moon

Every light source instantly extinguished until it becomes total darkness for the caster. Magic emitting light and celestial objects does not provide light for 10 minutes for anyone around the caster in 8 Space, including the caster and it’s allies.

XIX The Sun

Gain the Sun Condition Start glowing like the Sun. Blind everyone but yourself. Clear: 1 minute.

XX Judgement

Everyone around the caster in 8 Space who killed another creature in the last day roll 1d10 and suffer this many true damage, including allies and the caster.

XXI The World

Choose any spell. Draw an extra card. You can cast the spell immediately using any card you have. This does not cost any extra AP.
