Conversions:

  • STR damage/lost equals to Wounds (same amount)
  • Any WIL damage becomes Drained (only 1)
  • Deprived = Drained
  • Any DEX damage becomes Exhausted (only 1)
  • Attribute save = Attribute roll
  • book = artifact which holds the spell (spirit)
