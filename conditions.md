## Wound

- Clear: 1 on Rest

## Drained

- -1 `WIL`
- Clear: all on Rest but drawing spell cards is effected before the clear.

## Focused

- +1 `WIL`
- Clear: on Attribute roll or at will

## Exhausted

- -1 `DEX` rolls
- Clear: all on Rest

## Refreshed

- +1 `DEX`
- Clear: on Attribute roll or at will.

## Weakened

- -1 `STR`
- Clear: all on Rest

## Strengthened

- +1 `STR`
- Clear: on Attribute roll or at will.

## Hungry

- +1 Disadvantage on each Attribute or Damage roll.
- Clear: all after eating a meal

## Well-Fed

- +1 Advantage on the next Attribute or Damage roll.
- Clear: on roll or at will

## Damned

- +1 Disadvantage on Attribute or Damage rolls.
- Clear: time or rest

## Blessed

- +1 Advantage on Attribute or Damage rolls.
- Clear: time or at will

## Prone

- You can only crawl
- Melee attacks have +1 Advantage against you
- Ranged attacks have +1 Disadvantage against you
- Cannot flank
- 0 Slot (not tracked in Backpack)
- Clear: 1 AP to stand up

## Flanked

- Multiple enemies standing close threatening from multiple direction.
- Melee attacks has +1 Advantage against you
- 0 Slot (not tracked in Backpack)
