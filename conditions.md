## Wound

- Clear: 1 on Rest

## Prone

- You can only crawl, melee attacks +1 against you, ranged attacks -1 against you.
- Clear: 1 AP to stand up

## Drained

- -1 on `WIL` rolls.
- Clear: all on Rest but drawing spell cards is effected before the clear.

## Focused

- +1 on the next `WIL` roll.
- Clear: on roll or at will

## Exhausted

- -1 on `DEX` rolls.
- Clear: all on Rest

## Refreshed

- +1 on the next `DEX` roll.
- Clear: on roll or at will.

## Weakened

- -1 on `STR` rolls.
- Clear: all on Rest

## Strengthened

- +1 on the next `STR` roll.
- Clear: on roll or at will.

## Hungry

- -1 on each Attribute or Damage rolls.
- Clear: all after eating a meal

## Well-Fed

- +1 on the next Attribute or Damage roll.
- Clear: on roll or at will

## Damned

- -1 on Attribute roll or Damage rolls.
- Clear: 10 minutes

## Blessed

- +1 on Attribute or Damage rolls.
- Clear: 10 minutes or at will
