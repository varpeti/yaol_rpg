Major Arcana

varpeti

0 The Fool

Teleport the caster to the last room they left. If the room is more than 100m away, teleport 100m in that direction to the nearest safe spot.

I The Magician

Draw 5 cards, keep the lowest 2, burn the rest.

II The High Priestess

Summon an invulnerable, small, white-gold Pegasuscat. Each creature next to the Pegasuscat (1 Space) gets +1 Advantage for Attribute and Damage rolls. The Pegasuscat has 3 Speed, and can be commanded to move as a Small Action. Lasts for 10 minutes.

III The Empress

Roll 1d4. The caster suffers this as true damage; the closest ally gains this many HP (up to their maximum).

IV The Emperor

Gain a Golden Apple: When eaten, it removes 1 Wound Roll 1d10 and place it in the corresponding slot in your Backpack. If there is an Item on that slot, drop it to the floor. If there is a Condition on that slot, remove it.

V The Hierophant

The caster freezes in loud prayer to their god for at least 5 minutes. They are invulnerable until they finish the prayer, but they require air and are not immovable. If the prayer is interrupted, the caster takes 1 Wound.

VI The lovers

Choose 2 creatures; they gain the Lovers Condition:

Lovers

  • They cannot harm each other in any way. If they do, the harm automatically applies to the attacker instead of the victim.
  • Clear: 10 minutes.

VII The Chariot

The caster gains +3 Speed. Next time the caster moves, everyone within 1 Space moves as well, provided they are not blocked by walls or other living beings. Size does not matter.

VIII Justice

Within 8 Space of the caster, if any creature harms another, they take the same amount of damage themselves for 10 minutes. (0 Slot Condition)

IX The Hermit

Silence falls to the 8 Space area around the caster for 10 minutes. (Area effect)

X Wheel of Fortune

Roll on the d666 table. Immediately cast that spell. This does not cost any extra AP or card and automatically succeeds.

XI Strength

Everyone within 8 Space of the caster gains 3 Strengthened Condition

XII The Hanged Man

The caster’s body disappears and becomes a floating head; it cannot fly higher, but it can maintain its current height. It can float at normal walking speed. It can turn back to normal at will.

XIII Death

Choose a number between 3 and 10; everyone within 8 Space of the caster (including allies and the caster) takes this much true damage.

XIV The Tower

Everyone within 8 Space (including the caster and their allies) rolls 1d20 and teleports directly up, stopping at the ceiling.

XV The Devil

Make a deal with the Devil:

  • Permanently lose 1 slot from the Backpack and gain +1 Attribute
  • Permanently lose 1 Attribute and gain +1 slot in the Backpack

XVI Temperance

Caster receives a Hungry Condition and a Holy Fasting Condition.

Holy Fasting

  • Cannot eat or drink for a day. Receives +2 STR, +1 WIL and +1 DEX. If you eat, you die.
  • Clear: 24 hours

XVII The Stars

Burn all your cards and draw as many +1.

XVII The Moon

Every light source is instantly extinguished, leaving the caster in total darkness. Magic emitting light and celestial objects are blacked out only for a moment.

XIX The Sun

Gain the Sun Condition

Sun

  • Start glowing like the Sun. Blind everyone but yourself.
  • Clear: 1 minute.

XX Judgement

Everyone within 8 Space of the caster who killed another creature in the last day rolls 1d10 and suffers this much true damage (including allies and the caster).

XXI The World

Choose any spell existing in the World. Draw an extra card. You can cast the spell immediately using any card you have. This does not cost any extra AP.
