## The Honeyed Tongue

- A small lump of amber with something trapped inside, might be a bee, might be a word (1 slot)
- **Say it sweet**: Hold it under your tongue while you speak, and up to `NUMBER` listeners believe whatever you say next, certain it was their own idea, for `DICE` minutes, until something makes them doubt it.
- Recharge (1): Get someone to genuinely thank you for something you didn't actually do.

## The Troll's Fingerbone

- A thick, gnarled finger bone, still faintly damp and smelling of wet stone (1 slot)
- **Touch of the troll**: Touch anything living with it and moss creeps out, covering `NUMBER` spaces, thick enough to hide in or trip on, lasting `DICE` minutes. It is extremely flammable.
- Recharge (1): Let a wound close on its own, no bandage, no stitches, no treatment at all.

## The Thief's Shadow

- A strip of shadow cut from a hanged man's silhouette (1 slot)
- **Just walk away**: Unfold it and it drapes over you whole, no eye can follow you, not invisible, just always somehow missing, letting you slip through any crowd or past any guard, for `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Leave it laid flat somewhere no light ever reaches, for a week.

## The Dormouse Skull

- A tiny skull no bigger than a walnut, always faintly warm as if something's still dreaming inside (1 slot)
- **Close your eyes**: Hold it up and breathe out slow, everything within `NUMBER` paces that can sleep, does, dropping mid-sentence if it has to, for `DICE` minutes or until something wakes it.
- Recharge (1): Stay awake for a full day and night, then sleep somewhere genuinely unsafe.

## The Broken Wand

- Half a wand, snapped clean (1 slot)
- **Catch it**: Hold it up the moment a spell is cast within `NUMBER` paces and it swallows the spell whole. Only if `DICE` bigger or equal.
- Recharge (1): Let a hostile spell hit you clean, no dodge, no armor, no help.

## The Dream Ink

- A vial of ink that shimmers even in full dark and smells faintly of burnt hair (1 slot)
- **Sketch it real**: Dip a finger and draw anything no bigger than 1 meter across, it peels off the surface and stands there looking exactly as real as belief allows, for `DICE` days. It does whatever you drew it doing and nothing else.
- Recharge (1): Smash every mirror in a room without looking at your own reflection.

## The Amber Bead

- A bead of cloudy amber with something small frozen mid-struggle inside (1 slot)
- **Stay exactly there**: Hold it up and point at up to `DICE` creatures within `NUMBER` spaces, they and you lock solid, mid-breath until someone takes damage.
- Recharge (1): Stand completely still yourself, outdoors, until something lands on you and leaves on its own.

## The Unraveling Thread

- A single thread pulled from something holy (1 slot)
- **Pull it loose**: Press it against any spell, enchantment, or magical effect. It unravels clean, like it was never there, up to `DICE` times before the thread goes slack and useless.
- Single use - Can be cast with 3 dice.
