## Sparrow-bone Whistle

- A whistle carved from a sparrow's wishbone (1 slot)
- **Call the murder**: Blow it. `DICE` crows tear out of nowhere, each do one violent or useful thing a crow can do, then burst to feathers. Last `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Leave a dead bird at a crossroad

## The Tin Bell of the Drowned Hour

- A church bell shrunk to fit a pocket (1 slot)
- **Drown the Hour**: Ring it. It rings without a sound anyone can name. For `DICE` minutes, everything in `NUMBER` radius moves like it is under water.
- Recharge (1): Leave the bell in a bell tower for a day.

## The Crooked Key

- An iron key bent at an angle no lock ever cut for it. It is small but surprisingly heavy (2 slot)
- **Open What Shouldn't**: Turn against anything solid, and it cease to exists for `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Leave it in somewhere it was never meant to be opened for overnight.

## The Folded Shadow

- A square of cloth black as a closed eye (1 slot)
- **Step where the light doesn't**: Press it flat against any shadow and fold yourself, plus up to `DICE` others touching you, into it. Step out of any other shadow within `NUMBER` spaces, within `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Seal it in true darkness, no light at all, for a full night.

## The Wormwood Seed

- A single black seed that never sprouts on its own, always faintly warm (1 slot)
- **Make it grow**: Push it into bare earth or a crack, and `DICE` roots violently burst up within `NUMBER` spaces.
- Single use - Can be cast with 3 dice.

## The Red Thread of Connection

- A spool of read thread (1 slot)
- **Tie a connection**: Get a `DICE` meter of thread, and press the ends to two different things. The thread connects them, and they cannot be further from each other in any way for `NUMBER` of days.
- Recharge (1): Soak the spool in blood under the moon for a night.

## The Last Snowflake

- A single snowflake sealed in a glass bead no bigger than a pearl, never melts (1 slot)
- **Bury the road**: Crush the bead and a blizzard tears out of nowhere, blinding everything and burying the ground within `NUMBER` spaces for `DICE` minutes. Deals `NUMBER` frost damage each minute.
- Single use - Can be cast with 3 dice.

## The Frozen Tear

- A single tear, frozen solid, hung from a thread and never melting on its own (1 slot)
- **Seal in glass**: Touch it to anything no larger than `NUMBER` meters across and ice swallows it whole, perfectly preserved exactly as it was, until the cube takes `DICE` damage and shatters.
- Recharge (1): Hold it against something that is freezing to death, until the cold finally takes them.

## The Sunjar

- A jam jar filled with trapped daylight, glowing faintly even at midnight (1 slot)
- **Let it out**: Unscrew the lid and the trapped daylight floods `NUMBER` spaces like high noon for `DICE` minutes. Anything looking straight at it as it opens is blinded.
- Single charge, rechargeable, can be cast with 3 dice: Leave the jar open under direct sun in the summer for a full daylight.

## The Charcoal Stub

- A stub of burnt wood (1 slot)
- **Draw it true**: Sketch any tool or item on a flat surface. Not bigger than a tool you can use with both hands. It peels free of the surface and becomes real. Crumbles to ash after `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Burn a tool or item to ashes which was useful to you in the past.

## The Hanging Needle

- A sewing needle on a length of horsehair thread, hanging crooked unless held just right (1 slot)
- **Point true**: Draw your blood with the needle, and name what you're looking for. For `DICE` minutes it strains toward it. It points to a random direction if it is not found up to `NUMBER` kilometers.
- Recharge (1): Let it hang untouched, completely still, for three full days.

## The Drowned Locket

- A small locket that won't open, filled with seawater that never sloshes (1 slot)
- **Borrow the depths**: Press it to someone chest and their lungs forget air for `DICE` minutes, breathing water as easily as air. But they no longer can breath air.
- Recharge (1): Drop it to the bottom of water deeper than it's ever been, and dive down after it yourself.

## The River Stone

- A smooth flat stone, always damp no matter how dry the air around it (1 slot)
- **Skip the surface**: Throw it across any water and a path follows wherever it skips, solid enough to stand and fight on, stretching up to `NUMBER` spaces and holding for `DICE` minutes before it sinks.
- Recharge (1): Let it sink to the bottom of moving water, and dive down to retrieve it yourself, no tools.

## The Boar's Tusk

- A yellowed tusk worn on a cord, always warm like it's still angry (1 slot)
- **Wear the boar's fury**: Bite down on it and your blows hit like a charging animal for `DICE` minutes, +`DICE` damage to every blow. But you can't stop swinging at the nearest thing until the fury runs out, friend or not.
- Recharge (1): Walk away from a fight you could have won.

## The Grasshopper's Hindleg

- A dried leg knotted to a boot lace, twitching whenever you stand still too long (1 slot)
- **Leap like nothing's there**: Stomp once and you launch `NUMBER` spaces, straight up or any direction. Must chain `DICE` leaps before you can land. It does not help with the landing.
- Recharge (1): Crush a dozen grasshopper with your hand.

## The Gopher's Tooth

- A curved yellow tooth that smells faintly of turned earth (1 slot)
- **Open the ground**: Press it into dirt and the earth swallows itself, opening a pit `NUMBER` paces wide and just as deep beneath whatever's standing there. Slowly fills back in on its own in `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Bury it a full body's depth in the ground and leave it overnight.

## The Bent Hoop

- A small iron ring, warped like it's been pulled through itself (1 slot)
- **Fold the path**: Throw it and it hangs wherever it lands, with a second, identical hoop opening up to `NUMBER` spaces further along the same line. Any projectile passing through either ring exits the other at the same speed and direction for `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Throw it through a real doorway, both directions, without touching it twice.

## The Saint's Knucklebone

- Smooth as a river stone, said to have belonged to someone who never lost a fight (1 slot)
- **Share the luck**: Press it into the palm of up to `DICE` willing allies, but not yourself. They get a boon: The next roll each of them makes will land where they want.
- Recharge (1): Give it to someone in worse trouble than you, and never claim it back.

## The Doorstone

- A flat black slab the size of your palm, somehow always heavier than it looks (2 slot)
- **Set it true**: Press it flat against any opening no bigger than `DICE` cubic meter across and stone grows out from it to seal the gap completely. Immovable and indestructible for `NUMBER` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Brutally kill an enemy with a blunt weapon of your choice, then leave the slab in the gore for an hour.

## The Bat-skin Pouch

- Stitched together from a hundred small wings, always smells faintly of a cave (1 slot)
- **Become the swarm** (boon): Pull it over your head and you scatter into a swarm of tiny bats for `DICE` minutes, slipping through anything that isn't sealed and shrugging off any attack that isn't an area effect. Spread up to `NUMBER` paces and still pull back together as yourself, push past that and you stay scattered forever. You can deal `DICE` damage if the swarm is together.
- Recharge (1): Sleep hanging upside down somewhere dark, undisturbed, for a full night.

## The Tattered Kite

- A small kite of bone and cloth that never needs wind to fly (1 slot)
- **Catch the gust**: Let it go and a wind picks up around you, strong enough to push anything unbraced, including yourself, if you choose, `NUMBER` paces, lasting `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Fly it in a heavy storm until the cord snaps, then tie it back together.

## The Last Coal

- A single ember kept in a tin box, never cools, never needs air to keep burning (1 slot)
- **Burn true**: Touch it and an object of your choice within `NUMBER` paces catches fire. Only that thing, never spreading, never needing fuel. Slowly burning for `DICE` minutes before only ash remains. To keep the burning object in hand needs a `WIL` saving throw.
- Recharge (1): Feed it something directly in the box, that actually mattered to you, and watch it burn.

## The Oathring

- A plain iron ring, worn smooth from being twisted in prayer (1 slot)
- **Hold the line**: Plant your feet and swear the oath aloud. For `DICE` minutes, you and every ally within `NUMBER` paces of you take half harm from anything meant to hurt them, so long as you don't take a single step back.
- Recharge (1): Forgive someone who actually wronged you, and mean it.

## The Patron's Eye

- A glass eye that doesn't match your eyes, and watches even when you don't (1 slot)
- **Open it and ask**: Press it to your palm and something elsewhere answers, striking whatever you're looking at, hard enough to knock it down, up to `NUMBER` paces away.
- Recharge (1): Tell it a secret you've never told anyone else.

## The Lodestone Knuckle

- A fingerbone wrapped in copper wire that always pulls toward metal (1 slot)
- **Let go**: Snap it between your fingers and every hand within `NUMBER` spaces forgets how to hold anything, including yours. The bane wares of in `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (3): Hold it through a serious fistfight without throwing a single punch.

## The Brassbound Boot

- A single iron-toed boot (1 slot)
- **Put your foot in it**: While wearing it, kick anything and it goes flying `NUMBER` paces, `DICE` times before the boot needs a breather.
- Recharge (1): Receive damage from a heavy kick.

## The Grave Ash

- Ash from a sanctified pyre that never blows away in any wind (1 slot)
- **Ward the threshold**: Scatter it in a ring up to `DICE` paces across, and the living dead cannot cross it, stand against it, or close on whoever's inside without breaking and fleeing, for `DICE` minutes.
- Recharge (1): Burn a corpse down to ash yourself, and mix it with the ash.

## The Sympathetic Knot

- Two loops of the same rope, tied through each other, impossible to pull apart by hand (1 slot)
- **Bind the two**: Loop one ring around anything and the other around anything else within `NUMBER` meters whatever happens to one, happens equally to the other for `DICE` minutes, good or bad: all of it shared.
- Recharge (1): Tie 100 ties.

## The Reversed Coin

- A coin stamped heads on both sides, but they're never the same face twice (1 slot)
- **Flip it**: Spin it in the air and two things within `NUMBER` paces swap one property until it lands.
- Recharge (1): Lose a fair bet, on purpose, without telling the other person.

## The Bear's Gallbladder

- A small dried sac on a leather cord, bitter enough to smell from a foot away (1 slot)
- **Swallow the pain**: Bite down on it and for `DICE` minutes every hit you take makes you hit harder, each wound adds +1 to your next blow, stacking, until the minutes run out or you drop dead.
- Recharge (1): Take a beating without fighting back, and still be standing at the end.

## The Seedpod Crown

- A dried crown of interlocked seedpods that rattle when the weather is about to change (1 slot)
- **Return it to root**: Set it on the ground and then stone cracks, roads split, floors buckle, and roots, vines, and thorns swallow everything within `NUMBER` spaces, wild and indifferent.
- Recharge (3): Plant a tree sapling and come back to water it every day for a year.

## The Splinter of the True Door

- A sliver of ancient wood, warm as skin, from a door that was opened and never closed (1 slot)
- **Speak through it**: Hold it to your lips and speak a prayer, the wound it touches closes (`DICE` Attribute healing), the poison it touches eliminates, the fever it touches breaks.
- Recharge (1): Sit with a dying friend and stay until it's over.
